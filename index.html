DESIGN THINKING PROBLEM

Why should an older safe product cost the same as a newly manufactured one?

Customers may receive products with different remaining shelf lives while paying the same price. FreshPrice creates a transparent pricing system based on product freshness.

  • 🟢 Fresh product → Regular price
  • 🟠 Near-expiry safe product → Discount
  • 🔴 Expired product → Not for sale
  • 🚫 Poor-quality product → Blocked
